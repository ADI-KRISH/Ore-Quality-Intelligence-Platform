Explain the stage/module: $ARGUMENTS

1. Walk through the data flow in plain language: what comes in, what each function does, what goes out, and why it's done that way.
2. Point out the 3 lines or decisions most likely to be asked about in an interview, and why.
3. Name one thing that would break if we moved from this CSV to a real plant historian.
4. Then ask me 5 questions, one at a time, that I should be able to answer without looking at the code. Wait for my answer to each before the next. Correct me honestly if I'm wrong or vague.
